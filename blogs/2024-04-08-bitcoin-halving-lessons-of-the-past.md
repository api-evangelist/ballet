With the 2024 Bitcoin Halving rapidly approaching, a retrospective look at the 2020 Halving may offer valuable lessons for navigating profitably through the upcoming event. Bitcoin continues to generate immense attention with its quadrennial Halvings, which reduce its currency issuance rate and significantly impact the market every four years. The 2020 Halving left a lasting imprint on the ecosystem, affecting everything from market volatility and mining operations to transaction fee dynamics, institutional investment, and public engagement.
